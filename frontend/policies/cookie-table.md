# Cookie Table
- access_token
  - Used to recognize you as a logged-in user
  - Essential
  - 1 hour persistence
- refresh_token
  - Keeps you logged-in by renewing your session automatically
  - Essential
  - Week-long persistence
- session
  - Used to maintain a secure login process
  - Essential
  - Session
- bc:config
  - Stores configuration details to connect you to your Bitcoin Lightning wallet
  - Provider: bitcoin-connect (third-party service)
  - Functionality
  - Indefinite Persistence
- saveSearchHistory
  - Stores your preference for saving your search history
  - Functionality
  - Year-long persistence
- searchHistory
  - Stores your recent searches so you can recall them later
  - Functionality
  - Year-long persistence
