About

The Music Podcast Player was originally a capstone project developed by a team of six students at Ball State University during the 2025-2026 school year. It was developed in collaboration with Dr. Willey, who provided the vision for the project as well as guidance and feedback. Additionally, the capstone class was taught by Professor Adithya Kulkarni, who encouraged the team and instructed them in software development best practices. The team is listed below in alphabetical order.

  • Aidan Bensch
  • Brody Roth
  • Evan Greenfield
  • Reylon McCollum
  • Spencer Smith
  • Timothy Gulley

The project is under the MIT license, and the source code can be found on GitHub.
