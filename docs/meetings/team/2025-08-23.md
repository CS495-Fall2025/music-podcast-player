## Discussion Points

### Regular Meeting Times

We tried to set up meeting times during the meeting, and at a few points found times
that worked for most people, but there would always be at least one person (out of the
five present at the meeting) who wasn't avaliable. We decided to all send our
avaliability after the meeting and figure it out then. If we cannot find two times that
work for everyone, we will do three meetings with four attendees at each.

### Team Roles

We decided to rotate the role of scrum master each meeting, and rotate/temporarily
assign the other roles dependant on the needs of the project as we go. (For instance,
we may not always need UI design every week, and sometimes we may need more developers.)

### Six Issues (docs structure?)

We split the documentation structure listed in Canvas into six issues, each assigned to
one person. Most of the markdown files will be empty for now until we know their purpose
and have more information. In order to get empty directories in the repository, we will
create placeholder files. (To my knowledge it is not possible to commit an empty
directory to a repository.)
