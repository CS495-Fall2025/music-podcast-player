# Music Podcast Player Authors

This document is a list of all authors who have made contributions to the Music
Podcast Player codebase. This list does not strictly reflect copyright, refer to
Git history to see contributions made by individual authors.

## Capstone Team

Music Podcast Player was originally a capstone project developed at Ball State
University in collaboration with our mentor, Dr. Robert Willey, who provided the
initial vision for the project as well as valuable feedback and guidance
throughout the project. We'd also like to thank Professor Adithya Kulkarni, who
led our capstone class, taught us software development best practices, and
encouraged us throughout our journey.
